## 🛠️ How Does It Work?

topowall works by fetching real digital elevation model (DEM) data for your chosen area. This data represents the height of the terrain at thousands of points. The program then calculates contour lines – the same lines you see on hiking maps – that connect points of equal elevation. These lines are drawn using your graphics card's GPU to produce crisp, smooth curves instantly.

The result is a mathematically precise representation of the landscape that also looks like modern, minimalist art.

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

- **Choose interesting terrain** – Mountainous areas like the Alps, Andes, or Himalaya produce dramatic contour patterns. Flat regions create sparse, simple designs.
- **Adjust contour interval** – Smaller intervals (e.g., 50 meters) create dense, intricate patterns. Larger intervals (e.g., 500 meters) give a cleaner, bolder look.
- **Experiment with colors** – Classic looks include dark lines on light backgrounds or light lines on dark backgrounds. For a unique twist, try neon colors on black.
- **Save presets** – Once you find a style you love, save it as a preset so you can apply it to different locations instantly.

**Problem:** Preview is blank or rendering fails.
**Solution:** Your GPU driver may be outdated. Update your graphics driver from the manufacturer's website (NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel).

**Problem:** The download is slow.
**Solution:** This can happen during high-traffic periods. Try again later, or use a download manager for better speed.
